ABOUT THE ROLE

Colaberry has helped 5,000+ people future-proof their careers in data & AI since 2012. As a Data Analytics Instructor, you'll lead live cohorts through SQL, BI, and applied AI coursework — coaching career-switchers from their first query to a job-ready portfolio. You'll shape curriculum, mentor 1:1, and keep our classrooms warm, inclusive, and hands-on.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
Teach live SQL, Power BI, and Python sessions to cohorts of 20–30 learners, on campus and remote.
Review portfolio projects weekly and give specific, encouraging feedback tied to hiring-manager expectations.
Improve curriculum each cohort — refresh datasets, labs, and AI-assisted workflows with the education team.
Mentor learners 1:1 on study plans, interview prep, and confidence — many are veterans and career-switchers.
Track cohort progress in our LMS and flag learners who need extra support early.
WHAT YOU'LL BRING
3+ years working with SQL and at least one BI tool (Power BI, Tableau) on real business problems.
Experience teaching, mentoring, or onboarding others — formal classroom experience not required.
Plain-language communication: you can explain a JOIN to someone who has never written code.
Nice to have: Python for analytics, LLM/AI-assisted tooling, or prior bootcamp/adult-education experience.
BENEFITS
Medical, dental & vision coverage
401(k) with company match
15 days PTO + education holidays
Annual learning & certification budget
