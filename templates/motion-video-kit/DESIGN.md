# Colaberry — Motion video design system

Brand kit for generating Colaberry videos. Colaberry helps people future-proof their careers in data & AI — warm, inclusive, proven since 2012 (5,000+ careers launched).

## Personality
Warm, not loud. Confident without hype. Clarity over decoration: content and numbers lead. Gentle, purposeful motion — never showy, never bouncy.

## Color
- Background (light): #FFFFFF, soft off-white #F7F7F7
- Background (dark): #101010 near-black
- Primary accent — Cherry red: #FB2832 (the constant; CTAs, highlights, key words)
- Secondary — Leaf green: #77BB4A (growth, success moments)
- Tertiary — Berry blue: #367895 (trust, data, links)
- Text: #1A1A1A on light, #FFFFFF on dark; muted gray #4A4A4A
- Rule: one accent leads per scene (usually cherry); never bluish-purple gradients; never recolor the logo cherries.

## Typography
- Headlines & body: Roboto (Bold for display, Regular for body). Sentence case.
- Numbers / metrics / code: Roboto Mono.
- Never Inter/Arial. Quicksand is logotype-only — never set text in it.
- Big, generous headlines; short lines; lots of whitespace.

## Logo
- Full-color horizontal lockup on white/light scenes; white mark on dark, photo, or cherry backgrounds.
- Clear space equal to the cherry-mark height on all sides. No shadows, gradients, or recoloring.
- End card: logo centered on white (or white mark on cherry #FB2832) + one-line CTA.

## Motion style
- Easing: ease-out for entrances, ease-in for exits. Never linear, never hard bounces.
- Speed: quick and gentle — 140–360ms per element transition; hero moments up to 560ms.
- Recipes: fade & rise 14–28px for entrances; stagger sequenced items ~70ms apart; overlays scale 0.96→1; exits fade & fall fast.
- Rounded shapes: pill buttons/badges, 16–24px card radii, soft shadows.
- Data moments: clean chart animations (bars grow, counters tick up) using berry blue sequential tones; cherry↔leaf for diverging.
- No infinite looping motion on content; shimmer only for loading states.

## Voice & copy
- Speak to "you." Warm, encouraging, plain language. Sentence case everywhere.
- Lead with proof: 5,000+ careers, since 2012, no degree required, scholarships, veterans support.
- CTAs: "Apply now", "Enroll today", "Start free".
- Never emoji. Never hype-speak ("revolutionary", "unleash").

## Scene patterns
1. Hook: bold Roboto headline on white, one cherry-red key word, fade-and-rise.
2. Proof: big Roboto Mono counters (5,000+ careers · since 2012) ticking up, staggered.
3. Story: soft rounded cards / UI snippets sliding in, berry-blue data accents.
4. CTA end card: logo + "Apply now" pill button in cherry red, white background.
