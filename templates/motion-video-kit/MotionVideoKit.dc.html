2 · Format

3 · Brand rules — paste as-is

Colors: white #FFFFFF / off-white #F7F7F7 backgrounds (dark scenes #101010 ok). Cherry red #FB2832 is THE accent — CTAs, key words. Leaf green #77BB4A for growth moments; berry blue #367895 for data/charts. One accent leads per scene. Never purple gradients.
Type: Roboto Bold headlines, Roboto Regular body, Roboto Mono for numbers. Sentence case, short lines, generous whitespace.
Logo: attached files only — full-color horizontal on light scenes, white mark on dark/cherry. Never recolor or add effects.
Motion: gentle ease-out fade-and-rise entrances, staggered lists, counters ticking up, bars growing. 150–400ms per element. Never bouncy, never linear, no spinning 3D.
Shapes: pill buttons/badges, 16–24px rounded cards, soft shadows.
Voice: warm, encouraging, speak to "you". No emoji, no hype-speak.
