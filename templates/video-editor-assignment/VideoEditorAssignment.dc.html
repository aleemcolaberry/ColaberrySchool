4 · Editing style — what "viral" means here

Hook (0–2s)

Pattern interrupt + the boldest claim on screen before second two. No logo intro.

Cut rate

New visual every 1–3s, cut on the beat. Punch-ins, speed ramps, whip transitions.

Captions

Big kinetic text, 3–5 words per line, key words popped in cherry red.

Sound

Music with a clear beat, SFX layer (whooshes, clicks, risers), ducked under VO.

Proof beat

Numbers hit hard — counters, stat pops: 5,000+ grads, $80K, 98%.

End card (last 3s)

Clean stop: logo + "Apply now — training.colaberry.com". Nothing else moving.

5 · Materials & ground rules

  • Footage: screen-record any pages of training.colaberry.com; royalty-free b-roll (Pexels, Pixabay, etc.) is fine — cite sources.
  • Logos: use the attached brand kit only (full-color on light, white mark on dark/cherry). Never recolor or add effects.
  • Brand guardrails: Roboto for text, cherry red #FB2832 as the lead accent, sentence case, no emoji, no hype-speak.
  • Music/SFX: licensed or royalty-free only. AI voiceover is allowed if narrated.
  • Original work: your edit, made for this assignment. Any editor works — Premiere, Resolve, CapCut, AE.
