04Principles

Five beliefs
that break ties.

When a design decision is ambiguous, these settle it — in order.

01

Inclusive by default

Accessibility, plain language and generous touch targets are requirements, not extras. If everyone can't use it, it isn't done.

44px targets · AA contrast · plain words
02

Warm, not loud

Soft shapes, gentle motion, friendly copy — confidence without hype.

soft radii · 140–360ms motion · no shouting
03

Clarity over decoration

We teach data, so content and numbers lead. Ornament earns its place.

numbers first · ornament earns its place
04

One system, many makers

Semantic tokens and reusable components let a marketer, a developer and an AI produce the same brand.

245 tokens · 20 components · one voice
05

Consistent & constant

The cherry accent never changes; foreground inverts with the background. Trust is built by repetition.

the cherry never changes
